KALAVEDA WEBSITE
=================

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- downloads/kalaveda.apk (put your final Android APK here)

HOW TO USE:
1. Put the final APK at downloads/kalaveda.apk.
2. Replace the placeholder phone, WhatsApp, email and location in index.html.
3. Upload the whole kalaveda_website folder to your web hosting.

The website is a normal static website. It does not require Firebase or a database.
